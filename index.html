Common pain points
  • ✓ Last-minute cancellations and no-shows
  • ✓ Overbooking / double bookings across therapists
  • ✓ Hard to manage opening hours, breaks, holidays
  • ✓ Too many chats to confirm bookings manually
  • ✓ Payments & tracking feel messy (who paid, how, when)
  • ✓ Difficult to see customer history and repeat visits
Recommended flow
  1. 1 Customer chooses massage type + therapist + time
  2. 2 System checks availability and confirms instantly
  3. 3 Send a friendly reminder via Email
  4. 4 Take payment (cash/card/online) and mark status
  5. 5 Review reports and improve schedules weekly
